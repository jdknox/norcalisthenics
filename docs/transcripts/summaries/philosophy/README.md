# philosophy/

Theme: a single transferable minimalist framework applied identically
to fitness, language, reading, and business — smallest sustainable
input (~40 min/week training, 5 min/day language), practicing at
genuine failure/threshold rather than volume, and diminishing returns
as proof "fittest in the room" beats "fittest in the world." Long
timelines (5-10 years) are framed as liberating, not daunting;
motivation and appearance are unreliable guides, replaced by objective
metrics, process-efficiency diagnosis, and daily visualization of a
specific end-state. Subtraction (fewer goals/exercises/inputs) plus
scheduled consistency and external guidance are what separate people
who reach a goal from those who quit during the slow, unrewarding
early "phase 1."
