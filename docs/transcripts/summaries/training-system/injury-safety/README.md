# training-system/injury-safety/

Theme: injury risk comes from training volume overloading slow-
recovering connective tissue, not from intensity — minimal-volume/
max-intensity training plus an instantly-reducible bodyweight "zero"
fallback is safer. Injuries get managed by redirecting training, not
stopping entirely.

## Files
- `24-09-24_injury-prevention-summary.md` — Two-tissue model: volume damages connective tissue; intensity (not volume) grows muscle.
- `25-04-28_max-intensity-safety-summary.md` — Real risks are uncontrolled load and excess volume, not intensity; practice a "zero" fallback.
- `26-04-20_training-injuries-summary.md` — Three personal injury case studies (tricep tear, golfer's elbow, sternum pain) and his fixes.
