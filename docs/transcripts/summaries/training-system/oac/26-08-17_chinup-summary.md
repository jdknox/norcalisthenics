# How to build chinup strength insanely fast (beginner friendly)
2026-08-17

[In this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dMAtdUvoP-8) Woods argues "you actually don't need to do a lot of chin-ups" to get their full benefit.

Below is a paraphrased summary, not a transcript.

## Core principle
- Treats chin-up ability as a byproduct of vertical-pull maximum strength, not a skill to rehearse — train near-maximum force output, not volume or endurance.
- Once maximum strength is well beyond what a bodyweight chin-up requires, reps become trivially easy — so the actual goal is raising the strength ceiling, not practicing the movement itself.
- Strength gained this way is described as largely permanent (like muscle mass) — it returns quickly even after time off.

## Method — one-arm assisted work
- Train one arm at a time; use the free (assisting) arm and/or feet to remove exactly enough load to allow movement.
- Find the limit: attempt with no assistance at all, then add just enough foot assistance to move (finds the concentric limit); alternately, hold at the top and remove assistance until forced into a controlled negative (finds the eccentric limit).
- Ceiling used: a full one-arm chin-up — chosen because it provides large headroom beyond a standard two-arm chin-up, so difficulty can keep scaling up indefinitely as strength improves.

## Rep standard
- Full range of motion required for a rep to count: start in a dead hang, arms fully extended overhead, hips hanging straight down, pull until the wrist touches the chest.
- Avoid pulling "out of plane" (tilting back) — only step feet up onto an elevated surface for the top-half assist once you can no longer reach the floor at all, to avoid training a different movement pattern.

## Dosage
- As little as 1 rep per arm, once per week, is stated as sufficient to stimulate progress.
- Total working time: roughly 1–2 minutes, alternating arms; stop either when it's no longer enjoyable or when strength clearly starts to drop off.
- Suggests pairing with a separate horizontal-pull session 3–4 days apart in the week to cover the other pulling pattern.
