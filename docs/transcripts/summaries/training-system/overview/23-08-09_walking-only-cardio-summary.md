# Why Walking Is The Only Cardio You Need
2023-08-09 (blog post, no video)
https://jackhwoods.com/why-walking-is-the-only-cardio-you-need

Below is a paraphrased summary, not a transcript. Consistent with his separately summarized "Cardio is for your mind, not your body" video, but this earlier post states the argument more tersely, with a specific numeric prescription.

## Core argument
- States cardio (running, cycling, swimming, intervals, etc.) is irrelevant to physique outcomes (getting lean, muscular, defined) — those outcomes come entirely from strength training (building muscle) and diet/calorie balance (losing fat). Explicitly frames traditional cardio-for-fat-loss as a common but ineffective strategy people eventually abandon (either from lack of results, or by escalating effort further with still no results).
- Carves out one specific exception: walking, prescribed at a flat 10,000 steps/day, as his sole "cardio" practice — framed as important for mood, energy, and general wellbeing (not for the physique-related outcomes covered by strength + diet).

## Stated two-part "recipe"
1. A consistent strength training routine built around long-term strength goals (referenced elsewhere in his content as the six/five basic movements) — stated to require as little as half an hour a week if done correctly.
2. 10,000 steps/day of walking.
- Explicitly gives permission to skip any other cardio entirely without guilt, provided these two things are in place — while noting that other cardio is fine to do if genuinely enjoyed for its own sake, just not as a means to a physique or "being healthy" end in itself.
