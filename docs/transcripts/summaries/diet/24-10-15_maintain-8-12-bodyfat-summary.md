# How to easily maintain 8-12% body fat year-round
2024-10-15

[In this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TdDT4wXZiWg) Woods states his method: "Here's how you can easily maintain 8-12% body fat year round."

Below is a paraphrased summary, not a transcript. Core content (muscle mass first, protein/whole-food appetite regulation, get-lean-once model) overlaps heavily with several other summarized nutrition videos — this one's distinct contribution is the specific target ranges stated and the male/female framing.

## Target ranges given in this video
- Men: 8–12% body fat cited as the achievable, healthy, sustainable "lean with visible definition" range.
- Women: 16–20% cited as the equivalent range — describes the same process applying identically, just with different target numbers reflecting typical physiological differences (states women in this range won't have visible abs the way men do, but will achieve a comparable toned/athletic look).

## Two-principle model (consistent with his other nutrition content)
1. **Build muscle mass first** — without it, reaching ~10% body fat looks "skinny," not the desired athletic/toned look, and isn't sustainable since it likely sits below a person's comfortable weight range.
2. **Fuel adequately** (protein target 1.8 g/kg bodyweight/day, whole-food-dominant diet) so appetite naturally regulates — once muscle mass is adequate and nutrient needs are met, the body's own appetite signals are framed as keeping weight at the target range without ongoing conscious restriction.

## Getting there initially
- States the fat-loss phase to reach the target range is a one-time process (though it may take multiple 4–12-week deficit cycles depending on starting body fat) — once reached and maintained via principles 1 and 2, no further intentional dieting is described as necessary.

## Personal note specific to this video
- States this has been his own sustained range for multiple years and describes it as requiring no ongoing "grind" — framed explicitly against the common assumption that visible leanness year-round necessarily requires constant restriction or sacrifice of normal eating/social life.
