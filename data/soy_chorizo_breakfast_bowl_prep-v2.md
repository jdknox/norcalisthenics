## 2. Complete 5-Day Grocery List (Metric)
* **Soy Chorizo:** 1 standard package (340g — e.g., Trader Joe's or El Burrito Soyrizo)
* **Butternut Squash:** 1 container pre-cubed fresh squash (510g–625g — available at Whole Foods) or 1 bag frozen diced squash (454g)
* **Black Beans:** 1 standard can (400g–425g, yields ~250g drained)
* **Clover Greek Yogurt:** 1 tub (500g total, 70 kcal / 12.8g protein per 100g)
* **Large Eggs:** 10 eggs (2 per morning)
* **Feta Cheese:** 125g crumbled feta
* **Cherry Tomatoes:** 375g (stays crisp for all 5 days without getting watery)
* **Avocados:** 2.5 medium avocados (~250g edible flesh total)
* **Fresh Lemon & Garlic:** 2 lemons + 2–3 garlic cloves (for the tangy yogurt sauce)
* **Pantry Staples:** Olive oil, sea salt, fresh herbs (cilantro, dill, or parsley), and optional harissa paste or chili crisp

---

## 3. Sunday Batch Prep (15–20 Minutes, Zero Scale Needed)

### Step A: Cook the Hot Base (Choose Your Method)
* **Option 1: Cast-Iron Skillet (15–18 mins)**
  1. Heat **10 ml olive oil** in a large cast-iron skillet over medium heat. Add **500g–600g pre-cubed squash** in a single layer with a pinch of salt. Sear undisturbed for 4–5 minutes, then stir occasionally for 8–10 minutes until tender and caramelized. *(Tip: Cover with a lid for 3 minutes halfway through to steam the centers faster.)*
  2. Push squash to the edges. Peel the casing off the **340g soy chorizo**, crumble it into the center for 2 minutes, then fold in the **drained black beans** (plus **1 tbsp harissa paste**, if using) for 1 final minute.
* **Option 2: Air Fryer (15 mins)**
  1. Line the basket with a silicone liner or raised-edge parchment bowl to catch seasoned oil drips.
  2. Toss **500g–600g pre-cubed squash** with 5–10 ml olive oil and salt. Air fry at **200°C for 12 minutes**, shaking halfway.
  3. Crumble the **340g soy chorizo** and scatter **drained black beans** directly over the hot squash. Air fry at **190°C for 3–4 minutes** until sizzling.
* **Divide:** Stir the hot squash, chorizo, and beans together and divide evenly across **5 airtight containers**.

### Step B: Whisk the 5-Day Tangy Yogurt Sauce (2 Minutes)
In a jar or container, whisk together:
* **500g Clover Greek yogurt**
* **60 ml fresh lemon juice** (~1.5 to 2 lemons)
* **2 finely grated garlic cloves** (or 1 tsp garlic powder)
* **15 ml olive oil**
* **45 ml cold water** (thins the yogurt into a pourable cafe-style drizzle)
* **1/2 tsp sea salt** + chopped cilantro, dill, or parsley

---

## 4. Morning Assembly & Zero-Scale Visual Cues (3 Minutes)
1. **Reheat Base:** Microwave 1 container of the chorizo, bean, and squash mix for 90 seconds.
2. **Cook Fresh Eggs:** While the base heats, scramble **2 whole eggs** in a nonstick pan (pre-cooked eggs turn rubbery by day 3).
3. **Top Using Visual Cues:**
   * **Tangy Yogurt Sauce:** Spoon **1/5 of the jar** (~2 heaping soup spoons / ~100g) over the warm base.
   * **Feta Cheese:** 1 generous two-finger pinch (~25g).
   * **Cherry Tomatoes:** 4 to 5 whole or halved cherry tomatoes (~75g).
   * **Avocado:** 1/3 of a medium avocado (~50g), sliced fresh (or pre-sliced with lemon juice).
   * **Finish:** Drizzle **1–2 tsp chili crisp** over the top for crunch.
