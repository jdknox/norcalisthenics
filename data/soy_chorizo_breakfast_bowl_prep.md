# 5-Day Zero-Scale Soy Chorizo Breakfast Bowl Prep

A high-protein, metric-based breakfast prep designed around standard package sizes so you never have to weigh food daily.

---

## 2. Grocery List (5 Days)

* **Soy Chorizo:** 1 standard 340g package
* **Black Beans:** 1 standard 400g can (yields ~240g drained)
* **Butternut Squash:** ~750g–850g raw cubed squash (shrinks to ~500g after roasting)
* **Eggs:** 10 whole eggs
* **Clover Greek Yogurt:** 500g tub (70 kcal / 12.8g protein per 100g)
* **Feta Cheese:** 125g block or crumbled tub
* **Cherry Tomatoes:** 1 punnet (~375g) — *lasts all 5 days without turning mealy*
* **Avocado:** 2 medium avocados (plus lemon juice to prevent browning)

---

## 3. Zero-Scale Batch Prep & Morning Workflow

### Sunday Prep (15 Minutes)
1. **Roast the Squash:** Toss cubed butternut squash with minimal oil/salt and roast at 200°C for 25–30 minutes until tender.
2. **Cook the Base:** Remove plastic casing from the **340g soy chorizo** and crumble into a hot skillet for 3–4 minutes. Drain and rinse the **can of black beans**, stir them into the chorizo for 1–2 minutes to absorb the spices, and turn off the heat.
3. **Divide by Eye:** Split the chorizo-bean mixture and roasted squash evenly across **5 airtight containers**.

### Daily Morning Assembly (4 Minutes)
1. **Reheat Base:** Microwave 1 container of chorizo, beans, and squash for 60–90 seconds.
2. **Cook Fresh Eggs:** Scramble **2 whole eggs** in a pan while the base reheats (takes 3 minutes and avoids rubbery reheated eggs).
3. **Add Visual Toppings (No Scale Needed):**
   * **Clover Yogurt:** 2 heaping soup spoons (~100g)
   * **Feta:** 1 generous two-finger pinch (~25g)
   * **Cherry Tomatoes:** 4 to 5 whole tomatoes (~75g)
   * **Avocado:** 1/3 of a medium avocado (~50g)

### One-Tap App Logging
Save the exact daily row weights from Section 1 as a custom **"Saved Meal"** in your tracking app once. Log the entire bowl with one tap each morning.
